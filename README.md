# Mubashir Ishimwe | IT Support & Networking

I’m an IT support technician in Ottawa. I use this site to share what I’ve worked on, from getting loaner laptops back into service at Algonquin College to building desktops for resale.

The homepage pairs a green-screen terminal illustration with my work history. The archive has nine case studies you can search by tool or filter by platform. You can also compare two projects side by side, like my Veeam restore lab and the backup plan I later designed for Proxmox.
