The brief

A client’s connected status answers only one question about a wireless network. I built a lab sensor to see how channel use, signal behaviour, and associations fit together.

My approach

  1. Set up the sensor

    I configured a Kismet sensor for monitoring wireless activity in the lab.

  2. Read the radio context

    I examined access points, client associations, channels, and signal behaviour together.

  3. Document the observations

    I translated the captured telemetry into structured findings and investigation notes.

What I documented

The sensor gave me a view of 802.11 activity that I could describe in technical reports, including how observations related to channel and signal context.

Lab note

A device identifier alone gives an incomplete picture of what is happening on a channel.
