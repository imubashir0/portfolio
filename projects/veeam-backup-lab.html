The brief

I wanted to verify the recovery path for systems and endpoints, including the repositories and policies behind a backup job.

My approach

  1. Set up repositories

    I configured Veeam Backup & Replication with multiple repositories and retention policies.

  2. Connect endpoints

    I implemented agents to include endpoints in the backup workflow.

  3. Exercise recovery

    I tested backup and restore procedures to check how the configured recovery path behaved.

What I verified

The lab included tested backup and restore procedures. I later applied that experience to a separate backup design for a Proxmox home lab.

Lab note

A completed backup job and a successful restore answer different operational questions.
